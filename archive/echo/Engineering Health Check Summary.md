# Engineering Health Check Summary

## Overview
A recurring retrospective for engineering teams focused on development experience and codebase satisfaction, separate from product/feature discussions.

## Key Characteristics
- **Frequency**: Every two weeks (adjustable based on team needs)
- **Duration**: 30 minutes initially, can be reduced with practice
- **Facilitator**: Senior engineer from another team, acts as facilitator not problem-solver
- **Format**: Hand voting (1-4 finger votes or Roman up/down votes) on subjective topics

## Purpose
Provides a structured way for engineers to step back from their IDE and discuss codebase satisfaction objectively as a team. Helps identify patterns and issues through tracked metrics over time.

## Topics Covered
1. **Developing and Changing Code**
   - Ease of adding features
   - Code understandability
   - Code quality under active development
   - Overall project code quality

2. **Automated Development Tests**
   - Test suite effectiveness
   - Test suite consistency
   - Test quality for active development
   - Overall test quality

3. **Other**
   - Pipeline confidence
   - Technology stack comfort
   - Pairing effectiveness

4. **Overall**
   - Project trend (up/sideways/down)

## Process
1. Facilitator walks through prompts
2. Participants vote using finger or Roman votes
3. Results recorded in spreadsheet for trend tracking
4. Outliers briefly discussed (not solved)
5. Action items assigned to address later

## Key Principles
- Keep sessions brief (30 min)
- Focus on identifying problems, not solving them
- Facilitator must be empathetic listener from outside the team
- Avoid having direct managers facilitate
- Teams can customize topics and frequency as needed
