## Why

A facilitator who archives a topic to try a leaner session — and later decides it should come back — has no way to bring it back today. TOPIC-005 (Restore Archived Topic) has been fully drafted in `requirements/design/REST API Contract.md` since before TOPIC-004 shipped, and the Topic Management screen `remove-topic` (#51) built already has a working archived-topics section with per-row provenance sitting right there — it just has no button. Every removal a facilitator makes right now is a one-way door in practice, even though nothing about the data model requires that: `topics.status` is a two-value enum, and archiving never touches a topic's name, prompt, vote type, or history. The topic flexibility guardrail this feature area exists to protect — "teams should be able to add, remove, and adapt topics... without losing the baseline" — stays half-built as long as removing is safe to try and reinstating isn't.

This matters now for the same reason it mattered when `remove-topic` shipped: the whole point of making a topic easy to retire is that a facilitator can experiment without it being a real decision. A one-way remove isn't a safe experiment, it's a commitment, and a facilitator who suspects that will just stop trying it — quietly narrowing what teams are willing to adjust, for no reason the tool needs to impose.

## What Changes

- **`POST /api/v1/teams/:teamId/topics/:topicId/restore` (TOPIC-005) — new endpoint.** Transitions a topic from `archived` back to `active`, appended at the end of the current display order, not its prior position — see design.md Decision 3 for the full reasoning (the `topics_team_order UNIQUE (team_id, display_order, status)` collision-avoidance argument). Reuses the check-ordering cascade, error envelope, advisory-lock pattern, and shared `checkStandingFacilitatorOrAdminAuthorization` function TOPIC-004 already established, extended with the two checks specific to restore: topic-already-active (`422 TOPIC_ALREADY_ACTIVE`) and the append-position race, guarded by the same per-team `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext(teamId))` TOPIC-004 and TOPIC-003 both already use. **Corrects, before it ships, the same authorization gap `remove-topic` found and fixed for TOPIC-004:** the contract as drafted authorizes TOPIC-005 with a facilitator-only check and no `application_admin` branch, a repeat of the FR-8.2 `[HARD]` violation Marcus Delgado's review caught for TOPIC-004 — fixed here by reusing the corrected function as the third caller, not re-deriving the mistake a fourth time.
- **Provenance parity: `restored_by`/`restored_at` join `archived_by`/`archived_at`, and archiving provenance is preserved through restore, not cleared.** A facilitator looking at a topic's history a week later should be able to tell it was gone for a while without reading the audit log. Both fields are dedicated, nullable columns, matching the precedent `archived_by` already set rather than overloading `updated_at`.
- **Restore affordance and confirmation dialog on the existing Topic Management screen's archived-topics section.** One button per archived row, one confirmation dialog naming both the topic and the team (matching `remove-topic`'s established confirmation pattern), noting that historical data will be restored. No new screen, no bulk restore, no history timeline — the archived-topics section `remove-topic` already built gets one more action added to it.

**Deliberately out of scope: the trend-view gap signal.** See "Scope Decision" below.

## Scope Decision: Trend-Gap Signal Deferred to a Follow-Up Change

Exploration and design work for this change originally included a trend-view gap signal: a marker in the EM trend view (`EmTrendDataPage.tsx`, `TREND-001`/`TREND-002`'s response) indicating that a topic was intentionally absent for a stretch of sessions, derived from `session_topics` absence. Rachel Okonkwo's executive review of this proposal recommended splitting that work out into its own change rather than shipping it bundled with restore. I'm accepting that recommendation, as Devon Calloway, for three reasons that all independently hold:

1. **The use case's own Notes say so.** `08 - Topic Management - Use Cases.md`'s Notes for this use case state that the trend-view gap treatment "should be designed in conjunction with the Trend Dashboard feature set." That's the requirements document itself flagging a different owner and timeline — not a detail for a topic-restore change to settle unilaterally. I should have caught this scoping signal during exploration rather than needing an executive review to surface it.
2. **Restore has no dependency on it, in either direction.** A facilitator can restore a topic, see it reappear in the active list, and see its historical vote data preserved, entirely independent of whether the trend view marks the gap. The acceptance criterion that wants "some gap treatment" is satisfied by a correct, if currently unmarked, absence of interpolated data — not blocked on the marker shipping simultaneously.
3. **It can't be demonstrated end-to-end today regardless.** No shipped endpoint populates `session_topics` at session creation (#175, still open) — the gap signal could only ship unit-tested against synthetic fixtures, with no way to verify it against a real session. Deferring it to land alongside the Trend Dashboard work (which will need to solve #175-adjacent problems anyway to be useful at all) avoids shipping something nobody can currently watch work.

**This is a deliberate scope cut, not a silent drop.** The trend-gap signal is real, scoped, designed work — `exploration-notes.md` §2 and the prior draft of `design.md` Decision 6 worked out the derivation (from `session_topics` absence, not provenance timestamps, so it generalizes across multiple archive/restore cycles) and the response shape (`gaps: Array<{ afterSessionNumber, beforeSessionNumber }>` on `EmTopicTrend`). That reasoning isn't wasted; it's the starting point for the follow-up change, once a Trend Dashboard owner is named. I'm recommending the team lead file a GitHub issue for it, referencing this change and the use case's Dependencies note, rather than filing one myself mid-review — the issue should name whoever ends up owning Trend Dashboard once that's assigned, which isn't my call to make.

**Consequence for this change's scope:** `manager-team-association` is no longer a capability this change touches — `EmTopicTrend`, `EmTopicSessionDataPoint`, and `EmTrendDataPage.tsx` are all out of `Impact` below. The restore confirmation dialog's copy (`topic-management-screen`) no longer promises that "the gap will be visible in trend views," since that's no longer true at the time this change ships — it states only that historical data is restored.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `restore-topic`: The `POST /api/v1/teams/:teamId/topics/:topicId/restore` endpoint (TOPIC-005) — authorization (reusing the shared standing-facilitator-or-admin check), the full check-ordering cascade including `TOPIC_ALREADY_ACTIVE`, the advisory-lock-guarded append-position reposition, provenance (`restored_by`/`restored_at`, preserving `archived_at`/`archived_by`), and audit posture (`topic.restored`).

### Modified Capabilities
- `topic-management-screen`: adds a "Restore" action to the archived-topics section already shipped by `remove-topic`, plus the single-step confirmation dialog for it. Active topic list, remove action, and lock/customization behavior are untouched.
- `topic-customization-lock`: `GET /api/v1/teams/:teamId/topics/all` (TOPIC-002)'s `archived[]` entries gain `restoredBy`/`restoredAt` (nullable), alongside the existing `archivedAt`/`archivedBy`. No change to this endpoint's authorization model or any other field.

## Impact

**Code:**
- `packages/backend/src/routes/topics.ts` — new `POST .../restore` handler, alongside the existing TOPIC-003/TOPIC-004 handlers in the same file, reusing `checkStandingFacilitatorOrAdminAuthorization`, `checkTeamExists`, `checkCustomizationLockGate`, and `applyTimingFloor` verbatim.
- `packages/backend/src/auth/standing-facilitator-access-helper.ts` — no change; TOPIC-005 becomes this function's fourth caller (TOPIC-002, TOPIC-004, and now TOPIC-005).
- `packages/shared/src/types/topic.ts` — new `RestoreTopicResponse` type; `GetAllTopicsResponse.archived[]` gains `restoredBy`/`restoredAt` (nullable).
- `packages/frontend/src/pages/TopicManagementPage.tsx` — a "Restore" action and confirmation dialog on the archived-topics section, following the existing `RemoveTopicState`-style local component state pattern.
- `packages/backend/src/auth/audit-logger.ts` — `"topic.restored"` added to `AuditEventName`.

**Not touched by this change (deferred, see Scope Decision above):** `packages/backend/src/routes/em-views.ts`, `packages/shared/src/types/em-views.ts`, `packages/frontend/src/pages/EmTrendDataPage.tsx` — the trend-gap signal originally scoped here is deferred to a follow-up change.

**Schema:** one additive migration — `topics.restored_by uuid NULL REFERENCES users(id)`, `topics.restored_at timestamptz NULL`. No backfill (no topic has ever been restored by any shipped endpoint).

**Requirements documentation:**
- `requirements/design/REST API Contract.md` — TOPIC-005's `Authorization` line (`:818`) and `403` error-table row (`:843`) corrected together, the same way TOPIC-004's were: the customization lock moves from `403` to `409`; `application_admin` is added to the authorization line; a `422 TOPIC_ALREADY_ACTIVE` row is added.
- `requirements/use cases/08 - Topic Management - Use Cases.md` — "Re-Add a Previously Removed Topic"'s first Note (position: append vs. restore prior slot) is resolved, citing the drafted contract and design.md Decision 3. The second Note (trend-gap treatment) is *not* resolved by this change — it's updated to point at the follow-up change recommended above instead of being left as an open question with no owner.

**Known Limitations (pre-existing, not introduced by this change):**
- The use case's acceptance criterion "the re-added topic can be reordered within the active list after reinstatement" is currently unsatisfiable for *any* active topic, restored or otherwise — TOPIC-006 (Reorder) doesn't exist in the codebase yet, and no reorder endpoint or capability exists anywhere in the shipped product. A restored topic becomes an ordinary active topic and will be reorderable the moment TOPIC-006 ships, same as any other active topic; there's no restore-specific blocker. This gap predates this change and predates `remove-topic`/`add-custom-topic` as well; it is not this change's job to close, and TOPIC-006 remains a separate future issue (see Impact → Not touched).
- The use case's acceptance criterion "after reinstatement, the topic appears in the active topic list and is included in the next session" is only partially demonstrable today. The "appears in the active topic list" half ships and is spec-covered in this change. The "included in the next session" half cannot be demonstrated end-to-end for *any* topic, active or restored, because no shipped endpoint populates `session_topics` at session creation (#175, still open, filed by `remove-topic`). See `specs/restore-topic/spec.md` for the explicit scenario marking this half as blocked on #175.

**Not touched:** the no-manager-participation rule, the simultaneous reveal, the facilitator-from-another-team requirement, TOPIC-006/007 (Reorder, Annotate — separate future issues), TOPIC-003's still-unfixed FR-8.2 gap (tracked separately as #176, not reopened here), the `session_topics` snapshot-at-creation gap (#175, not this change's job to fix), and the trend-view gap signal (deferred to a follow-up change — see Scope Decision above).
