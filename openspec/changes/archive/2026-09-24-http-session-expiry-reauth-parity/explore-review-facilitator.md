# Facilitator Review — http-session-expiry-reauth-parity (Issue #146)

**Reviewer:** Priya Nair, Facilitator (Subject Matter Expert)
**Reviewing:** `exploration-notes.md` (Devon Calloway), against `DraftSessionHost.tsx`, `ReauthRequiredTreatment.tsx`, `SessionLobbyPage.tsx`, and my own two prior reviews in this area (`session-timeout-continuity` and `reauth-required-client-prompt`).

---

## Overall

The code-level read is careful and I checked the parts that matter to me directly: the `teams.ts` category inconsistency, the `onRequest`-hook centralization, and the `DraftSessionHost.tsx` gap are all real. I agree with the headline finding — a facilitator or participant who hits `session_expired` on one of these calls today gets "please try again," retries into the identical 401, and has no idea why. That's a real trap, not a theoretical one, and I'm glad this is closing it. I also agree with the inline-not-auto-navigate recommendation across the board, for the same reason I gave on the original banner: a page that yanks itself out from under someone because a background fetch 401'd reads as software running the room, not a room I'm in control of. I'm not relitigating that call.

Where this exploration is thin is the same place my reviews of its two predecessors were thin coming from Devon's chair: it verifies that the *facilitator's own screen* lands somewhere coherent, but it doesn't ask what the *room* experiences during the gap. That matters more here than usual, because the one new call site this issue adds — `DraftSessionHost.tsx`'s `advance` — is the single most attended-to moment in the whole inventory: it's the button I click while people are already waiting to get in.

## 1. This exploration never asks what participants experience while I'm stuck in reauth on the "open the room" button

I raised this same gap on `session-timeout-continuity` (Open Question 5 — "does the room stall") and got told it might be unnecessary. I still don't think it's unnecessary, and this issue reintroduces the identical question in a new spot: if my absolute lifetime expires in the window between clicking "Yes, open the room" and the response landing, I get routed to `ReauthRequiredTreatment` inline, click through, land back on `DraftSessionHost`, and have to click "Open the room" a second time. That's a clean, bounded interruption *from my side of the screen*, exactly as Devon describes.

But I checked the participant side of this join flow before writing this, and there isn't one yet — I grepped for it and the only thing that consumes a join token today is my own facilitator view (the "not yet joinable" badge on the draft screen) and `JoinErrorPage.tsx`. So I'm not asking this issue to build a "waiting for the facilitator" screen — that surface doesn't exist to design against yet, and inventing it here would be exactly the scope creep Devon's notes were careful to avoid elsewhere. What I want is for this to be *named*, not silently absent: whoever eventually builds the participant-facing join/waiting experience needs to know that "the facilitator clicked Open the Room and nothing happened for a while" is a real, if narrow, failure mode this change can produce, and that a silent stall there will read to a room of people staring at a join link exactly the way it did in my #133 review — indistinguishable from the tool being broken. I'd want a one-line forward note in proposal.md, the same way Devon named the pre-existing `ConnectionStatusBanner` gap on `SessionLobbyPage.tsx` without proposing to fix it.

## 2. Yes to inline-over-auto-navigate even on this button, specifically — but the CTA needs to be unmissable

Devon asks (implicitly, in the recommendation) whether inline treatment is right for every call site. My answer for `DraftSessionHost.tsx`'s `advance` specifically: yes, still inline, not auto-navigate — and I want to say why explicitly rather than let it be inherited by default, because this is the one call site where I could see someone arguing urgency cuts the other way ("people are waiting, just get her back in as fast as possible, skip the click"). I don't buy that argument. An auto-navigate under time pressure is worse, not better — it's the moment I'm least likely to be reading the screen carefully, and being yanked to a login redirect with no explanation while I still think I'm mid-click is more disorienting, not less, precisely because I'm rushed. A clear, click-through banner that tells me what happened and why is what keeps this feeling like a room I'm running rather than an app doing something to me. What I do want, given the urgency, is for the CTA to be visually impossible to confuse with the confirm dialog's own buttons on this page — `DraftSessionHost.tsx` already has a two-button confirm step ("Yes, open the room" / "Cancel") sitting right where this banner would need to render, and a rushed facilitator misreading a reauth prompt as another confirm click would be a bad moment to get wrong. I'd want to see this in a mock, not just described.

## 3. The confirm step's state doesn't survive the round trip — worth stating on purpose, not discovering later

I read `DraftSessionHost.tsx` directly. The "Opening the room lets participants join immediately, and cannot be undone. Continue?" confirm step is plain in-memory React state (`advanceState.phase === "confirming"`), not persisted anywhere. If my session expires *after* I've clicked "Open the room" but *before* I click "Yes, open the room" — i.e., while I'm reading the confirm copy — and the next thing that 401s is some other in-flight request, or I just come back to a stale tab, `returnTo` lands me back on the bare draft view, not back on the confirm step. That's the right behavior, and it's consistent with Devon's own point that `returnTo` gets you back to the page, not back to the in-flight action — I just want it said explicitly for this page, because this is the one call site in the inventory with a two-step confirm sitting in front of the POST, and I don't want an implicit assumption that reauth "resumes where I left off" to survive into a mock unchallenged.

## 4. The guarantee Devon names in passing is the thing that makes this button safe to interrupt at all — I want it stated as a requirement, not a footnote

Devon notes, correctly, that because `session_expired` is checked in `onRequest` before any route handler runs, a 401'd POST is guaranteed to have never executed server-side. For most of the call sites in this inventory that's a nice property. For this one specifically it's the thing that makes the confirm dialog's own promise ("cannot be undone") still true after an interruption. If a facilitator hits reauth mid-click on "Yes, open the room" and *isn't* sure whether the room actually opened before their session died, that's a materially worse experience than the generic-error trap this issue is fixing — it turns a clear failure into an ambiguous one, on the one action in this inventory whose whole warning copy is about irreversibility. I want this guarantee carried into design.md as a stated requirement this change relies on (Devon already frames it that way informally), not just an observation in exploration notes, because it's specifically load-bearing for whether I'd sign off on this call site.

## 5. On the vote-reveal question I was asked to think about: this doesn't touch it, and I want that said plainly

I was asked to consider whether the `DraftSessionHost.tsx` addition changes anything I perceive at the point of vote reveal. Having read the component: no. `advance` moves a session from `draft` to `lobby` — it's the room-opening action, not anything reveal-adjacent, and there's no live-voting or reveal UI wired into this page or reachable from it. This is consistent with Devon's own scope note that OR-1.7 and the live-voting UI don't exist yet. I don't have a concern here, but I want proposal.md to say this outright — "this change does not touch or come near the reveal path" — rather than leave it to be inferred, since "DraftSessionHost" and "facilitator control surface" are exactly the words that could get this issue mistakenly cited later as prior art for reveal-adjacent work it never touched.

## 6. One thing I checked and am satisfied with: the vote-loss sentence

`ReauthRequiredTreatment.tsx`'s `role` prop already omits the vote-loss sentence for `role="facilitator"` — this was the fix I pushed for in my `session-timeout-continuity` review, and it's already shipped and unconditional regardless of cause. `DraftSessionHost.tsx`'s new call site just needs to pass `role="facilitator"` like the existing facilitator-side consumer does. No new copy fork needed here. Good — one less thing to re-litigate.

## 7. Small gap: the initial-load error path, not just the button click

`DraftSessionHost.tsx`'s `loadFacilitatorState` (the GET on mount) has its own generic error branch (`"Unable to load this session."`), separate from `advance`'s. If I open a stale bookmark or an old tab to this page after my absolute lifetime already expired, that's the GET that 401s, not the POST — and I'd want the same `session_expired` routing applied there, not just on the button-click path. I don't think this is controversial, I just want it named as its own item in tasks.md so it isn't implicitly covered only by fixing `advance` and then missed at implementation.

## Bottom line

This is a small, mechanical fix and I don't think it needs a scope fight. I want three things carried forward explicitly, not left implicit: (1) a forward note that the participant-facing "room isn't open yet" experience — which doesn't exist as a UI surface today — will need to account for this same interruption once it's built, the same way I flagged the silent-stall gap on the WS side; (2) the no-partial-execution guarantee on `advance` stated as a design requirement, not an aside, because it's what keeps "cannot be undone" true through an interruption; (3) both of `DraftSessionHost.tsx`'s 401 paths (initial load and `advance`) covered, not just the one Devon traced in detail. None of this changes the shape of the fix Devon recommended — inline treatment, shared detection helper, `category`-keyed — I'm signing off on that shape. I do want a mock of the banner rendering over the confirm-dialog layout before I'd call the UX settled, same bar I've held every other surface in this ritual to.
